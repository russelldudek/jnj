## Company moment

- Johnson & Johnson reported Q1 2026 sales of $24.062B, up 9.9% reported.
- MedTech Q1 2026 sales were $8.636B, up 7.7% reported and 4.6% operational.
- MedTech growth was led by electrophysiology, Abiomed, Shockwave, and trauma.
- Johnson & Johnson announced an Enterprise Business Review for December 8, 2026.
- Johnson & Johnson has announced its intent to separate the Orthopaedics business, increasing the importance of operating continuity and clear process ownership during portfolio change.
- J&J MedTech describes its strategy as innovation at the intersection of biology and technology, with smarter, less invasive, more personalized treatments.
