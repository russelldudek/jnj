## Strategic argument

The campaign argues that a MedTech Digital Factory should not automate disconnected tasks. It should turn recurring order-to-cash friction into governed process products that make commitments explainable, exceptions visible, human authority explicit, and improvements reusable.

Because the original job page cannot currently be fully retrieved, `OTC = Order-to-Cash` is labeled as a working hypothesis for discovery rather than presented as confirmed job-description language.

## Actual mandate hypothesis

Turn fragmented, exception-heavy MedTech order-to-cash work into a governed portfolio of digital process products that improves customer service, operating visibility, control, adoption, and economic performance without destabilizing business continuity during significant portfolio change.

## Signature experience

**The Order Trace** is a visual path rather than a dashboard. One customer commitment moves through validation, availability, fulfillment, delivery, invoice, payment, and learning. Motion shows where work accumulates, where exceptions interrupt flow, and how a Digital Factory compresses recurrence rather than merely digitizing it.

## Role-derived motion

Three small signal packets move along the trace path at different phases. Selecting a lifecycle stage moves a larger decision marker to that point and updates the explanatory panel. With reduced motion, the packets stop at representative positions and the selected-state interaction remains fully usable.

## Meaningful interaction

The Friction Compression Simulator presents five exception scenarios. Choosing one updates:

- lifecycle stage;
- exception class;
- recommended intervention;
- retained human authority;
- proof metric;
- scaling decision.

This lets the visitor experience how the operating model changes a decision, not merely read a framework.

## Brand intelligence

- Official logo source: Johnson & Johnson corporate and MedTech websites.
- Usage decision: do not embed the official logo; use a restrained text reference and original Order Trace mark to avoid implying endorsement.
- Palette: J&J red, white, charcoal, clinical blue-green, muted violet, warm signal orange, and neutral gray.
- Typography character: modern, highly legible sans serif with editorial serif accents for strategic statements.
- Photography/illustration character: human and clinical; precise, science-led, less invasive, personalized.
- Geometry: fine rules, controlled curves, crisp modules, generous white space, high-contrast red accents.
- Motion character: precise, calm, path-based, sequential.

## Anti-clone review

The campaign avoids federated architecture, digital spine, control room, orbit, graph, dispatch queue, delivery weave, proving ground, continuity ledger, and nested operating envelope compositions. It also avoids the repeated four-pressure-card section fingerprint.

## Document system

- Resume: exactly two Letter pages, balanced page one, visible `View Cover Letter`, real PDF.
- Cover letter: exactly one Letter page, visible `View Resume`, real PDF.
- Interview brief: four deliberate pages.
- 120-day plan: three deliberate pages.
- OTC Exception Portfolio Canvas: two deliberate pages.

All web-only navigation and download controls are hidden from print and PDF output.
