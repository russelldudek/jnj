Choose and govern the intervention

4. Select the smallest useful intervention

InterventionUse whenSelected rationale
EliminateThe work is avoidable and the root condition can be removed.
StandardizeVariation is unnecessary and a clear rule protects value or control.
RedesignThe workflow or handoff creates the recurrence.
AssistContext or ambiguity requires human judgment with better support.
AutomateRules, data, ownership, controls, and exception paths are stable.
EscalateThe decision is consequential, rare, or outside delegated authority.

6. Make the portfolio decision

Scale

Value, behavior, controls, ownership, and transfer conditions hold.

Redesign / partner

Problem remains material, but intervention or ownership needs change.

Hold / stop

Evidence does not justify more burden, risk, or investment.

Reuse test

What transfers unchanged? What requires local configuration? What must remain local judgment? Which data, integration, control, and ownership conditions are required?

Portfolio rule: Do not scale a solution merely because the technology is reusable. Scale only when the decision logic, operating conditions, ownership, and retained value are reusable.
