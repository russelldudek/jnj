July 11, 2026Hiring CommitteeJohnson & Johnson MedTech

Director, MedTech Digital Factory - OTC

Johnson & Johnson MedTech is advancing at the intersection of biology and technology while managing a growing, changing portfolio. In that environment, the most important Digital Factory work is not simply implementing more automation. It is protecting customer commitments while turning recurring operational friction into governed, reusable capability.

My career has repeatedly placed me inside the work systems technology must improve. At Vape-Jet, I lead AI-first operating transformation across production, purchasing, quality, customer success, support, engineering issue flow, ERP/Odoo, CRM, collaboration systems, telemetry, and AI-assisted workflows. At Amazon, I led customer-backward execution in a 24/7 Prime operation supporting approximately five million second-day orders annually, building the visibility, standard work, escalation paths, and operating cadence needed to protect flow. At Compunetics, I led engineering, manufacturing, quality, customer delivery, and high-reliability technical programs where process controls and evidence could not be decorative.

That experience shaped the point of view I would bring to this role: a Digital Factory should convert recurring OTC friction into governed process products - making every order explainable, every exception visible, and every improvement reusable. The work begins by tracing the customer commitment across validation, availability, fulfillment, delivery, invoice, payment, and learning. It then classifies recurring exceptions before choosing whether to eliminate, standardize, assist, automate, redesign, or explicitly escalate them. Adoption and retained human authority are part of the operating model, not after-launch communications.

I would not claim to arrive with direct ownership of Johnson & Johnson's global MedTech OTC landscape or a major SAP transformation. I would arrive ready to partner with J&J's process, architecture, data, compliance, platform, and market experts while contributing a distinct strength: operator-level discipline in making work visible, connecting technical and business teams, establishing accountability, and proving whether change actually improves customer experience, control, economics, and sustained behavior.

The accompanying candidate vision at russelldudek.github.io/jnj/, along with the resume, interview brief, 120-day plan, and OTC Exception Portfolio Canvas, shows how I would approach the mandate. I would welcome the opportunity to test the hypotheses, learn the real decision boundaries, and discuss where the Digital Factory can create the greatest practical leverage.

Carpe diem,
Russell Dudek
